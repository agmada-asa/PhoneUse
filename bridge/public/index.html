Connect your phone

  1. Install and open the PhoneUse Android app.
  2. Show the QR code below, then select Scan pairing QR code on your phone.
  3. Enable accessibility, choose your blocked apps, then connect and enable control on the phone.
Accessibility blocked by a restricted setting?

On the phone, open Settings → Apps → PhoneUse → the three-dot menu → Allow restricted settings. Then return to Accessibility → Installed apps → PhoneUse and enable the service. PhoneUse also has a setup help button and an App info shortcut.
